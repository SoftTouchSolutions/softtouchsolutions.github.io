Notifications: common questions

I am not getting any notifications
  1. Open your phone Settings, find Almost Aboard, and check that notifications are allowed. On iPhone, also check that a Focus or Do Not Disturb mode is not hiding them.
  2. Check that the journey in the app shows Tracking, and is not Skipped or suspended.
  3. Check that your phone is online. On Android, battery saver modes can delay notifications; allow Almost Aboard to run in the background.
  4. If it still fails, email us with the journey details.
When does tracking start?

By default tracking starts 120 minutes before the scheduled departure. The time is shown on the journey screen.

Why did the train time change?

Times and platforms come from National Rail's live data feed and are updated as the train operators report changes. They can change at short notice, and a platform may only appear shortly before departure. Always check station displays and announcements.

Can I rely on the app to catch my train?

No. Almost Aboard is an aid, not a guarantee. Notifications can be delayed or missed, for example if your phone is offline. The data comes from third parties and may be wrong or late.

Does the app know my ticket?

No. Before you take a suggested alternative train, check that your ticket is valid on it. Ticket rules are set by the train operator.

How do I stop notifications for one journey?

Open the journey and choose to skip it or suspend it. To stop all notifications, turn them off for Almost Aboard in your phone Settings.

Your data

How do I see or export my data?

Open Settings in the app and choose Export my data.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings in the app, choose Delete my data and confirm. This removes your anonymous user ID, device and push tokens, trips, schedules and notification history from our server. Deleting the app alone does not do this, so delete your data first.

I deleted the app without deleting my data

Reinstall the app and use Delete my data if it still recognises you. Otherwise email us. Because there is no sign-up, we may not be able to identify which data was yours. Completed trips are deleted automatically after 30 days.
