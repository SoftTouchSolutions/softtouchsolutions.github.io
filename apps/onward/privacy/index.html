Almost Aboard: privacy policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Almost Aboard is a mobile app that watches a UK train journey you set up and sends you notifications about it, for example a delay, a platform or a cancellation. This policy explains what personal data the app and its server handle, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is: [CONTROLLER NAME AND ADDRESS].

Contact for anything in this policy: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

Almost Aboard is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by National Rail, Network Rail or any train operator.

2. What we collect

There is no sign-up. You do not give us your name, email address or phone number. When you first use the app, the server creates an anonymous user ID for you. We collect only what is needed to run the alerts you ask for:

DataWhat it is
Anonymous user IDA random identifier created by the server. It is not linked to your name or contact details.
Device and push tokensTechnical addresses that let the server send a notification to your phone, plus the device platform (iOS or Android), the app version, and when the app last contacted the server.
Trips and recurring schedulesThe journeys you add: the stations, the train you chose, the days it repeats, and whether a trip is skipped or suspended.
Last notified stateA record of the last status we told you about for a trip, so that we do not send you the same alert twice.
Notification logA record of which notifications were sent to your device and when.

Because a journey can reveal where you travel and when, we treat trips and schedules as personal data even though your identity is not attached to them.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your precise location. The app does not ask for location permission.
  • We do not use advertising, advertising identifiers or analytics or tracking software, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

6. How long we keep it

  • Alert history (the notification log) is deleted 30 days after it is created.
  • Device and push tokens are deleted when they stop working (the phone tells us the token is no longer valid), and when you delete your account.
  • Unused data: if you do not use the app, and no alert is delivered to your phone, for 180 days, your anonymous user ID and everything linked to it is deleted automatically. The 180 days start when we first store data for you and restart whenever you use the app or one of your saved journeys sends you an alert that is delivered to your phone.
  • If you use Delete my data (section 8), everything linked to your anonymous user ID is deleted.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see your data, have it corrected, have it erased, restrict or object to how it is used, and receive it in a portable form. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Because the app has no sign-up, we can only find data that belongs to a phone that still has the app installed and holds its anonymous ID. The in-app tools in section 8 are therefore the quickest and most reliable way to use your rights. If you need anything else, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL].

8. Export or delete your data in the app

  • Export: open Settings, then Export my data. You get a file with your trips, schedules and registered devices.
  • Delete: open Settings, then Delete my data, and confirm. This deletes your anonymous user ID, device and push tokens, trips, recurring schedules, last notified state and notification log from our server.

Deleting the app from your phone does not by itself delete the data held on our server. Use Delete my data first. If you can no longer open the app, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] and tell us as much as you can; we may not be able to identify your data without the app.

9. Children

Almost Aboard is a general travel tool. It is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them beyond what is described here. Because the app has no sign-up, we do not know a user's age.

10. Security

Data is sent between the app and our server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to the server is restricted.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or who we share it with, we will update this page and its effective date, and tell you in the app where the change is significant.
